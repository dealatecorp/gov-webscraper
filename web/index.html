BACKGROUND COLLECTION

Keep your workspace up to date.

Fetch latest tenders starts a real collection. Supabase checks automatically after a 15-minute gap, even when this browser is closed. Repeated clicks reuse the shared cooldown. You can adjust GeM page coverage here.

What is collected

GeM public bids and reverse auctions; CPPP, Defproc and HSL homepage tenders and corrigenda. CPPP includes both eProcurement and ePublishing. Homepage windows are limited; awards, cancellations and complete historical coverage are not yet collected.

Storage & access

Supabase stores notices and bookmarks. Tender PDFs are saved to a private Supabase Storage bucket. Collection continues through Supabase Cron while the browser is closed. The scheduler checks once per minute and dispatches when the 15-minute cooldown has elapsed.
